## 15-Second Hooks

### Hook 1 — The Problem

> My mom makes the art. Marketing it should not require becoming an ad agency.
>
> Muse Growth Studio lets artists pick a product, get a research-backed idea, edit the words in their own voice, and download a ready-to-post image and caption. No ad account setup. No auto-publishing. Just the pieces she needs.

### Hook 2 — The Gap

> Our saved analysis of SikhiArt's sampled catalog reported entry prints around $59.99 and larger prints up to $2,499.99; this is one competitor snapshot, not the entire market. Muse Spark spotted that gap from a public shop crawl. The artist then used Muse Image to stage her Ek Onkar print in a collector's room. One product, one researched idea, one post.

### Hook 3 — The Handoff

> She edits the headline. She compares the AI scene with her original painting. She downloads the image and caption and posts from her own Instagram. The CSV goes to Ads Manager for review. Nothing is published by the app. Nothing is billed in demo mode. She stays in control.

## 45-Second Social Teaser

> My mom makes the art. Marketing it should not require becoming an ad agency.

## Short Launch Post

> My mom makes the art. Marketing should not require an agency.
>
> Muse Growth Studio is an AI growth copilot for makers on Etsy, Shopify, and FineArtAmerica. Three flows, plain language, real Meta tooling:
>
> 🔍 **Competitor Intel** — Public shop crawl → Muse Spark finds what they sell and what they skip. Research brief with competitor gaps.
>
> 🎨 **Ad Creative Studio** — Pick a product → Muse Spark drafts audience + headline → Muse Image stages artwork in a collector room → seller edits copy → downloads ready-to-post image and caption.
>
> 📊 **Bid Advisor** — Upload a Meta Ads CSV → Python computes metrics → Muse Spark recommends budget moves with reasons.
>
> Everything runs on Muse Spark 1.3 and Muse Image 1.0. SAM 3.1 segments the artwork. ComfyUI is optional for advanced variants.
>
> Demo mode uses saved outputs — no API credits, no live calls. Download the CSV. Edit the checkout template. Post from your own account.
>
> My mom makes the art. She shouldn't need a marketing degree to sell it.
>
