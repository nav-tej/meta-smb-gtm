Privacy Policy

Muse Growth Studio is operated by n Plus Alpha LLC (nplusalpha.com). This policy explains in plain language what data the app collects, where it goes, and how to delete it.

1. Information we store and process

  • Account email & sign-in session: When you create an account, your email address and password authentication are managed via Supabase. Sign-in sessions use same-origin HttpOnly cookies (muse_access and muse_refresh). We do not use advertising trackers.
  • Saved drafts: While you edit on /today or /create, your draft is saved in your browser’s local storage on your device. When you sign in and click Save to account, your workspace draft is stored in our Supabase database tied to your account ID so you can load it on another device.
  • Product photos, prompts & AI generation: When you run a live AI generation or analysis, the product photo, text prompt, or public storefront information you submit is sent to Meta’s Muse Model API (api.meta.ai) to generate your copy, image, or brief (and to a configured ComfyUI server if optional variant workflows are used). Saved demo mode does not send your photos to external AI providers.
  • Generated images: When you create a new image while signed in, a copy is stored privately in Supabase Storage under your account so you can open it again later and on other devices. Only you can load it.
  • Weekly competitor digest: If you sign up for the digest email, we store your email address, the 1 to 3 public shop links you chose, and a hashed network identifier to stop abuse. We only send it after you confirm by email, and every digest has an unsubscribe link. We read only those shops' public pages.
  • Payment & billing records: Payments are processed by Stripe. n Plus Alpha LLC never sees or stores your full credit card number. We store your Stripe customer ID, subscription ID, billing period dates, and plan status in Supabase to provide your monthly runs.
  • Usage counts & rate limiting: We store your run count, operation reservation records, and a hashed network identifier (SHA-256 IP hash) in Supabase to enforce plan run limits and prevent automated abuse.
  • Product analytics: When enabled, we use PostHog to understand how the app is used: pages viewed, buttons clicked, and steps such as signing up, opening plans, starting checkout and completing a run. Signed-in visitors are identified by an internal account ID, never by email. We do not record your screen, we never send what you type, we remove sign-in links and other URL details before sending, and we honor your browser’s Do Not Track setting.

2. Service providers we rely on

  • Supabase: Account authentication, workspace draft and generated image storage, digest subscriptions, and usage/subscription accounting.
  • Stripe: Checkout, subscription management, and payment processing (statement descriptor: NPLUSALPHA.COM).
  • Meta (Muse API): AI text, vision, segmentation, and image generation when you run live Muse requests.
  • PostHog: Product analytics (usage events and page views; no session recordings).
  • Vercel: Application hosting and request delivery.
  • Resend: Email delivery for the weekly digest and its confirmation emails.

3. How to delete your data

  • On your device: Clear your browser’s site data/local storage for this site to remove device-saved drafts.
  • Your account & cloud workspace: Email help@nplusalpha.com from your account email address to request deletion of your account, saved workspace drafts, and associated usage records. This also deletes your stored generated images and any digest subscription. We complete deletion requests within 30 days. We keep payment and tax records for as long as the law requires (generally up to 7 years); Stripe keeps its own payment records under its policy.

4. Children

Muse Growth Studio is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has created an account, email help@nplusalpha.com and we will delete it.

5. Your privacy rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your personal information by emailing help@nplusalpha.com. We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising. California residents have these rights under the CCPA, and we will not treat you differently for using them.

6. Changes to this policy

We will update the date above when this policy changes and email account holders about material changes.

7. Contact us

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, email n Plus Alpha LLC at help@nplusalpha.com.
