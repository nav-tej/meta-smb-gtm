Cancellation & Refund Policy

Muse Growth Studio is operated by n Plus Alpha LLC (nplusalpha.com). Payments are processed by Stripe and appear on your card statement as NPLUSALPHA.COM.

1. Try before you pay

Every verified account includes a free trial of 3 Muse runs, plus unlimited access to the saved demo examples and local draft editor, so you can test Muse Growth Studio before purchasing a plan.

2. Plan pricing & canceling your subscription

  • Muse Starter: $12/month (billed monthly) or $108/year ($9/month billed annually) for 20 Muse runs per month.
  • Cancel anytime: You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account billing portal or by emailing help@nplusalpha.com.
  • What happens when you cancel: Your subscription will not renew again, and you keep full access to your remaining runs and saved workspace through the end of your current paid period.

3. Refunds

  • Billing errors or duplicate charges: If you were charged in error or billed twice, email help@nplusalpha.com and we will issue a full refund for the erroneous charge.
  • New subscriptions (unused runs): If you request a refund within 7 days of your initial purchase or renewal and have used 0 paid runs in that billing period, we will refund that payment in full [NAV TO CONFIRM: refund window (e.g., 7 or 14 days) and whether partial/pro-rated refunds are offered once runs are consumed].
  • Service outages: If a technical failure on our side consumed a paid run without delivering a result, contact help@nplusalpha.com so we can restore your run or refund the affected charge [NAV TO CONFIRM: credit restoration vs. monetary refund policy for failed runs].

4. How to request a refund or cancellation help

Email help@nplusalpha.com from the email address on your Muse account. Refunds approved by n Plus Alpha LLC are returned to your original payment method through Stripe (typically appearing within 5–10 business days depending on your bank).
